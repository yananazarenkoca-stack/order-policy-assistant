# Lost, Delayed, or Stolen Packages

## Delayed packages

If tracking hasn't updated for **7 business days** (domestic) or **15 business days** (international), contact us and we'll open a trace with the carrier.

## Package marked delivered but not received

1. Wait **2 business days**. Carriers sometimes mark parcels delivered early.
2. Check with neighbours, building management, and safe spots around your home.
3. Contact us within **14 days** of the delivery scan.

We file a claim with the carrier and resolve it within up to 10 business days.

## Signature required on large orders

Orders over **$500** require a signature on delivery. If you're not home, the carrier leaves a pickup notice.

## Replacement limits

For orders under $500 marked delivered but not received, we replace or refund **once per customer in any 12-month period**. Later claims within that period need a police report before we can help.

## Wrong address

We aren't responsible for packages sent to an address you entered incorrectly. You can change the shipping address within **1 hour** of ordering, or until the order is packed, whichever comes first.
