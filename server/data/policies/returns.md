# Returns Policy

> **Updated on September 1, 2026.** The standard return window changed from 60 days to 30 days. Orders placed before this date keep the old window — see "Orders placed before September 1, 2026" below.

Cedar Ridge Outfitters accepts returns of most items bought online at cedarridge.example or in our stores in Calgary, Canmore, and Kamloops.

## Return window

You can return an item within **30 days of the delivery date** (for in-store purchases, 30 days from the purchase date). The return window closes at 11:59 pm Mountain Time on the 30th day. For mailed returns, the carrier's drop-off scan must be dated within the window.

## Orders placed before September 1, 2026

Orders placed before September 1, 2026 keep the previous **60-day return window**, counted from the delivery date. The **order date**, not the delivery date, decides which window applies.

Example: an order placed August 30, 2026 and delivered September 4, 2026 can be returned until November 3, 2026.

## Condition of returned items

Returned items must be unused, unwashed, and have the original tags and packaging. You may set up a tent or unroll a sleeping bag indoors to check fit. Items that show outdoor use (dirt on footwear soles, a used stove, a slept-in sleeping bag) can't be returned and will be sent back to you. If an item failed because of a defect, see the Warranty policy instead.

## Items that can't be returned

- Final sale items (see Final Sale Items policy)
- Gift cards

## How to start a return

- **By mail:** go to Account > Orders > Start a return. You'll get a return authorization (RMA) number and a shipping label.
- **In store:** bring the item and your order number or receipt to any Cedar Ridge store. Online orders can be returned in store, and in-store purchases can be returned by mail.

## Return shipping cost

- **Within Canada:** we provide a prepaid Canada Post label. **$8.95 is deducted from your refund** for the label.
- The label fee is **waived** for: in-store returns, exchanges, Summit-tier Trail Points members, and items that arrived damaged, defective, or wrong.
- **International returns:** you arrange and pay for return shipping (see International Orders policy).

## Restocking fees

- **15%** on opened electronics: GPS units, satellite messengers, bike computers, and action cameras. Unopened electronics have no restocking fee.
- **20%** on oversized items (see Oversized Items policy).
- No restocking fee applies if the item is defective or arrived damaged.

## Holiday exception

Items ordered between November 15 and December 24, 2026 can be returned until January 31, 2027. See the Holiday Returns policy.

## After we receive your return

We inspect returns within 5 business days of arrival at our Calgary warehouse, then issue your refund as described in the Refunds policy. Trail Points earned on returned items are removed (see Loyalty Points policy).
