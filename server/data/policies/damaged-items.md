# Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items

## Reporting a damaged or wrong item

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or isn't what you ordered, report it within **7 days of delivery** at Account > Orders > Report a problem. Include photos of:

1. the item,
2. the outside of the box, and
3. the shipping label.

## What you get

You can choose a **free replacement** or a **full refund**, including the original shipping cost. No return label fee and no restocking fee apply.

- Replacements ship by **express at no charge** (standard to Zone 4) if the item is in stock.
- Items priced **under $40** don't need to be sent back. Keep, donate, or recycle them.
- Items $40 and over get a free prepaid return label.

This policy applies to **final sale items too**.

## Oversized items arriving damaged

For oversized freight deliveries (kayaks, canoes, bikes, etc.), inspect the item while the driver is there and **note any damage on the carrier's delivery receipt**. Report it to us within **48 hours** of delivery, not 7 days. If the damage isn't noted on the delivery receipt, we may not be able to approve the claim.

## Problems reported after 7 days

Defects found after the 7-day window are handled under the Warranty policy.
