# Warranty

## Cedar Ridge-brand warranty

Cedar Ridge-brand products have a **2-year warranty** against manufacturing defects, starting on the purchase date.

**Exception:** Cedar Ridge-brand items bought from the **Last Chance** clearance section have a **1-year warranty**.

The warranty still applies to final sale items, with the shorter period above for Last Chance purchases.

## Other brands

For other brands, the manufacturer's warranty applies. During the first **90 days** after purchase, we handle the claim with the manufacturer for you. After 90 days, contact the manufacturer directly.

## What the warranty covers and excludes

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship, such as failed seams, broken zippers on new items, and delamination.

It doesn't cover:

- normal wear and tear
- misuse, accidents, or alterations
- UV damage
- items used commercially or for rentals

## Making a warranty claim

Submit photos and your order number at Account > Warranty claim, or bring the item to any store. Proof of purchase is required. We assess claims within 10 business days.

## Warranty remedies

Depending on the defect, we **repair, replace, or issue store credit**. If the product is no longer sold, we issue store credit for the price you paid.

A repaired or replacement item is covered for the **rest of the original warranty period**. The warranty doesn't start over.

## Paid repairs

Our Canmore repair shop fixes items outside warranty. There's a **$15 assessment fee**, which is credited toward the repair if you go ahead with it.
