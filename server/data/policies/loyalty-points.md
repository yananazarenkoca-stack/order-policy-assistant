# Trail Points Loyalty Program

Trail Points is Cedar Ridge Outfitters' free loyalty program. Joining is automatic when you create an account.

## Earning Trail Points

- **Base tier:** 1 point per $1 spent.
- **Summit tier:** 1.5 points per $1 spent.
- Points are earned on the amount you pay **after** promo codes and Trail Points redemptions, before tax.
- No points are earned on shipping, taxes, or gift card purchases.

## When points become available

Points are **pending for 14 days after delivery**, then become available to spend. For in-store purchases, the 14 days start on the purchase date.

## Redeeming Trail Points

- **100 points = $5 off.**
- You need at least **200 points** to redeem.
- Points can cover at most **50% of the order subtotal**.
- Points can't be used to buy gift cards.

## Summit tier

You reach Summit tier when your **net spending** (after returns) reaches **$500 in a calendar year**. Summit status lasts until the end of the next calendar year. Summit benefits:

- 1.5 points per $1
- Free standard shipping on all orders to Zones 1–3
- Free return labels (the $8.95 fee is waived)
- Early access to sales

## Trail Points and returns

When you return an item, the points you earned on it are removed:

- **Pending points** are simply cancelled.
- **Available points** are taken off your balance.
- If you've **already spent** those points and your balance is too low, the missing points are **deducted from your refund at $0.05 per point**.

If you paid partly with points, that part of the refund comes back as points, with a new 90-day expiry.

## Trail Points expiry

Points expire after **12 months with no account activity** (no purchase and no redemption). Points restored from a refund expire 90 days after they're restored.
