# Preorders

Preorders let you reserve products that haven't been released yet. Preorder items are labelled **"Preorder"** with an expected release date.

## Payment for preorders

Preorders are **charged in full when you place the order**. This is different from backorders, which are charged when they ship.

## Preorder limits and discounts

- Limit of **2 units** of each preorder product per customer.
- **Promo codes can't be used** on preorders.
- Trail Points and gift cards can be used.

## Preorder price guarantee

If our price goes down after you order but before your preorder ships, we automatically refund the difference. After the item ships, the normal Price Adjustments policy applies.

## Preorder shipping

Preorders always ship separately and get **free standard shipping**, whatever the order total.

## Release delays and cancellations

If the release date slips, we email you. You can cancel a preorder at any time before it ships for a full refund.

## Returning preorder items

The return window starts on the **delivery date**. For the holiday return window, the **order date** of the preorder counts (see Holiday Returns policy).
