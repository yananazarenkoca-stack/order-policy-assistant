# International Orders

## Countries we ship to

We ship outside Canada to the **United States, United Kingdom, all European Union countries, Australia, New Zealand, and Japan**. Prices are always in Canadian dollars (CAD), and your card issuer handles the currency conversion.

## International shipping rates and times

| Destination     | Shipping cost | Free shipping over | Delivery           |
| --------------- | ------------- | ------------------ | ------------------ |
| United States   | $19.95        | $199 CAD           | 5–9 business days  |
| Other countries | $39.95        | $299 CAD           | 7–15 business days |

Express shipping isn't available for international orders.

## Duties and taxes

- **United States:** duties and taxes are **collected at checkout** (delivered duty paid). Nothing is due on delivery.
- **All other countries:** duties and taxes are **paid by you on delivery** (delivered duty unpaid).
- If you refuse an international package, we refund the order **minus the original shipping and the actual return shipping cost (minimum $30)**.

## Items we can't ship internationally

- Oversized items (kayaks, canoes, SUP boards, bikes, roof boxes)
- Dangerous goods: stove fuel canisters, bear spray, lighters
- Lithium batteries over 100 Wh
- Knives with a blade longer than 10 cm

## International returns

- The return window is the same: 30 days from delivery.
- You arrange and pay for return shipping. Please use a tracked service.
- **US orders:** duties and taxes paid at checkout are refunded for returned items.
- **Other countries:** duties and taxes paid on delivery **aren't refunded**.
- Exchanges aren't offered. Return the item and place a new order instead.
- Holiday returns: international packages must be **postmarked by January 31, 2027**.
