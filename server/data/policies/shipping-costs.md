# Shipping Costs

All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD). Shipping zones are defined in the Shipping Zones policy.

## Domestic shipping rates

| Zone   | Standard | Express       |
| ------ | -------- | ------------- |
| Zone 1 | $7.95    | $19.95        |
| Zone 2 | $9.95    | $19.95        |
| Zone 3 | $12.95   | $29.95        |
| Zone 4 | $24.95   | Not available |

## Free standard shipping

- **Zones 1–3:** free standard shipping on orders of **$99 or more**.
- **Zone 4:** free standard shipping on orders of **$199 or more**.
- The threshold uses the order subtotal **after** promo codes and Trail Points redemptions, and **before** tax. Paying with a gift card does **not** lower the subtotal, because a gift card is a payment method, not a discount.
- Express shipping is never free.

## Summit member shipping

Summit-tier Trail Points members get free standard shipping on **every** order to Zones 1–3, with no minimum. Zone 4 Summit members still need to reach the $199 threshold.

## Oversized item surcharge

Each oversized item (kayak, canoe, SUP board, bike, roof box) adds a **$49 surcharge**. The surcharge is **not** waived by free shipping or Summit status.

## Returns that affect free shipping

If you got free standard shipping and then return items so that the items you keep total less than $99 (Zones 1–3), the standard shipping cost for your zone is deducted from your refund. Summit members are exempt. See the Refunds policy for an example.
