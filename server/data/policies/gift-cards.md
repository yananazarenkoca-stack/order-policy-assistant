# Gift Cards and Bonus Cards

## Buying gift cards

- E-gift cards: any amount from **$25 to $500**, in $5 steps, delivered by email.
- Physical gift cards: $25, $50, $100, or $250, shipped free by standard shipping.
- Gift cards **can't be bought with another gift card or with Trail Points**.
- Gift cards are final sale and can't be exchanged for cash, except where the law requires it.

## Using gift cards

- Gift cards **never expire** and have no fees.
- You can use up to **5 gift cards** on one order.
- Buying a gift card doesn't earn Trail Points. Points are earned when the gift card is **spent**.

## Refunds on orders paid by gift card

If you return something paid for with a gift card, that part of the refund goes on a **new e-gift card**. We don't reload the original card.

## Lost gift cards

We can resend lost e-gift cards to the original recipient email. We replace lost physical cards only if you have the original receipt and the card number, and only for the balance that hasn't been spent.

## Promotional bonus cards

Some promotions give **Bonus Cards** (for example, "Spend $200, get a $25 Bonus Card"). Bonus Cards are **not** regular gift cards:

- They **expire 60 days** after they're issued.
- They can't be used to buy gift cards.
- **If you return items** and your qualifying purchase drops below the promotion's minimum (for example, below $200), we **cancel the Bonus Card** if you haven't used it. If you have used it, we **deduct its value from your refund**.
