# Order Cancellations

## Cancelling within 1 hour

You can cancel an order yourself **within 1 hour** of placing it, from Account > Orders > Cancel, as long as its status is still "Received". You get a full refund.

## Cancelling after 1 hour

After the first hour, contact support and we'll try to cancel. If the order is already **"Packed" or "Shipped"**, we can't cancel it. You'll have to return it under the Returns policy, and return fees apply.

If you refuse a delivery, we treat it as a return: the $8.95 return label fee is deducted and the original shipping isn't refunded.

## Partial cancellations

You can cancel individual items within the 1-hour window. If what's left in the order drops below the free-shipping threshold, standard shipping for your zone is charged.

## Backorders and preorders

Backordered and preorder items can be cancelled **at any time before they ship**, even after the 1-hour window. For preorders you get a full refund. For backorders the card authorization is released.

## Personalized items

Personalized items (embroidery, engraving) **can't be cancelled after 1 hour**, because production starts right away.

## Cancellations by Cedar Ridge

We may cancel an order because of a pricing error, suspected fraud, or stock we can't fulfill. You get a full refund. If we cancel because of a stock error, we also add **250 Trail Points** to your account.

## Refund timing for cancellations

Card authorizations are released within 3–5 business days. Completed charges are refunded within 5 business days.
