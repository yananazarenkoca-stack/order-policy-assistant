# Shipping Zones

All online orders ship from our warehouse in Calgary, Alberta. Your zone depends on your **shipping address**, not your billing address.

## Canadian shipping zones

| Zone   | Provinces and territories                                                   |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Zone 1 | Alberta, British Columbia, Saskatchewan                                     |
| Zone 2 | Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec                                                   |
| Zone 3 | New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland and Labrador |
| Zone 4 | Yukon, Northwest Territories, Nunavut                                       |

International destinations are covered in the International Orders policy.

## Zone restrictions

- **Zone 4:** no express shipping and no oversized freight delivery. Most Zone 4 parcels travel by air.
- **Dangerous goods** (stove fuel canisters, bear spray, lighters) ship by ground only, so they can go **only to Zones 1–3**. They can't be shipped to Zone 4 or outside Canada.
- **PO boxes:** standard shipping only (Canada Post). Express and oversized items need a street address.

## Store pickup

Free store pickup is available at our Calgary, Canmore, and Kamloops stores no matter what zone you live in. Pickup orders are usually ready within 1 business day (2–5 business days for oversized items).
