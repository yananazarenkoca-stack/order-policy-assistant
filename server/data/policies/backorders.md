# Backorders

Some out-of-stock items can still be ordered. These items are marked **"Backordered"** and show an estimated ship date.

## Payment for backordered items

Your card is **authorized** when you order but is **charged only when the item ships**. Gift card and Trail Points amounts are deducted when you place the order. Note that preorders work differently: they're charged in full when you order.

## Orders with in-stock and backordered items

In-stock items ship right away. Backordered items ship separately when they arrive, with **no extra shipping charge**.

## Backorder delays

If a backordered item is delayed **more than 30 days** past its original estimated ship date, we email you so you can keep waiting or cancel. If you don't reply within **7 days**, we cancel the item automatically.

## Price changes on backorders

You pay **the lower of** the price when you ordered and the price on the day it ships. Price increases don't affect you. Promo codes applied at checkout stay valid even if the promotion has ended by the time the item ships.

## Returns of backordered items

The return window starts on the date the backordered item is **delivered**, not the order date.
