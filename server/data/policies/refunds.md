# Refunds Policy

This policy explains how and when Cedar Ridge Outfitters refunds money for returned or cancelled items.

## Refund timing

- **Mailed returns:** refunds are issued within 5 business days after the return arrives at our warehouse and passes inspection. Your bank may take another 3–10 business days to show the credit.
- **In-store returns:** refunded at the till right away.
- **Cancelled orders:** see the Order Cancellations policy.

## Refund method

Refunds go back to the original payment method.

- **Split payments:** if you paid with more than one method, the refund is divided between them **in proportion to how much each method paid**.
- **Gift card portion:** refunded to a **new e-gift card**. We don't reload the original card.
- **Trail Points portion:** refunded as Trail Points. Restored points get a new 90-day expiry.
- **Cash (in store):** refunded in cash up to $200. Larger cash refunds are mailed as a cheque within 10 business days.

## Store credit option

Instead of a refund to your payment method, you can choose store credit and receive a **5% bonus** (for example, a $100 refund becomes $105 in store credit). Store credit is issued as an e-gift card and does not expire.

## What is refunded

- The price you actually paid for the item, after any promo code discount (see Promo Codes policy for how discounts are split across items).
- Sales taxes (GST/HST/PST) paid on the returned item.
- Original shipping charges are **not refunded**, unless the item arrived damaged, defective, or wrong.

## Deductions from a refund

Your refund may be reduced by:

1. **Return label fee:** $8.95 for mailed returns within Canada (waived in some cases — see Returns policy).
2. **Restocking fees:** 15% on opened electronics, 20% on oversized items.
3. **Lost free shipping:** if your order got free standard shipping and the items you keep total **less than $99**, we deduct the standard shipping cost for your zone (see Shipping Costs policy). This does not apply to Summit-tier members.
4. **Lost promo threshold:** if a promo code needed a minimum spend and the items you keep no longer meet it, the discount is removed (see Promo Codes policy).
5. **Spent Trail Points:** if points earned on the returned item were already spent, they are deducted from the refund at $0.05 per point (see Loyalty Points policy).

## Worked example

Zone 1 order: jacket $80 + pants $40 = $120, with free standard shipping. You mail back the pants.

- Pants refund: $40.00
- Return label fee: −$8.95
- Kept items ($80) are under $99, so Zone 1 standard shipping is deducted: −$7.95
- **Refund: $23.10**, plus the tax you paid on the pants.
