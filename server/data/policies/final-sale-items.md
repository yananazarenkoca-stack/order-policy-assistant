# Final Sale Items Policy

## Which items are final sale

Items marked **"Final Sale"** on the product page and receipt are final sale. This includes:

- Everything in the **Last Chance** clearance section (usually 40% off or more)
- **Opened** consumables: stove fuel canisters, freeze-dried meals, sunscreen, insect repellent, and water treatment tablets (unopened consumables can be returned normally)
- Personalized items (custom embroidery, engraving)
- Base-layer underwear and swimwear once the hygiene liner has been removed
- "Pre-loved" ex-rental and demo gear
- Gift cards

## What final sale means

Final sale items **can't be returned, exchanged, or price-adjusted**. The holiday return window doesn't apply to them.

## Exceptions for final sale items

- **Damaged on arrival or wrong item:** the Damaged Items policy applies, and you get a full refund or replacement even though the item is final sale.
- **Manufacturing defects:** warranty still applies. However, Cedar Ridge-brand items bought from **Last Chance** get a **1-year warranty** instead of the usual 2 years.

## Final sale status is set at purchase

An item's status on the date you **bought it** is what counts. If you paid full price and the item later moves to Last Chance, it stays returnable under the normal Returns policy. You can't get a price adjustment down to the Last Chance price, though (see Price Adjustments policy).
