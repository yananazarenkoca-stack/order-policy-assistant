# Price Adjustments

## Getting a price adjustment

If an item you bought drops in price on cedarridge.example or in our stores within **14 days of your order date**, we refund the difference. It must be the same item, size, and colour. Contact support with your order number.

Each item can be adjusted **once**.

## Backorders and preorders

- **Backorders:** you're automatically charged the lower price at shipping. After that, the 14 days count from the **ship date**.
- **Preorders:** the preorder price guarantee applies until shipping. After that, the 14 days count from the **ship date**.

## When we don't give price adjustments

- Final sale items, and price drops caused by an item moving into the **Last Chance** clearance section
- **Black Friday Week (November 27 – December 1, 2026)** sale prices, even if you bought the item within 14 days before. For example, an item bought November 20 that goes on sale November 27 doesn't qualify.
- Discounts from a promo code (a code isn't a price drop)
- Competitors' prices (we don't price match)

## How the adjustment is calculated

The adjustment is the difference between what you **actually paid** (after any promo code) and the new price. If the new price isn't lower than what you paid, there's no adjustment.

The refund goes to your original payment method. Trail Points earned on the item are reduced to match the new price.
