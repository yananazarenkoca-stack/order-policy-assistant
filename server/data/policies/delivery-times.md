# Delivery Times

## Order processing

Orders placed before **1:00 pm Mountain Time** on a business day (Monday–Friday, excluding Canadian statutory holidays) ship the same day. Orders placed later ship the next business day.

## Estimated delivery by zone

Delivery times are counted in business days from the day the order ships.

| Zone                | Standard  | Express       |
| ------------------- | --------- | ------------- |
| Zone 1              | 2–4 days  | 1–2 days      |
| Zone 2              | 3–6 days  | 2–3 days      |
| Zone 3              | 5–8 days  | 3–4 days      |
| Zone 4              | 7–14 days | Not available |
| United States       | 5–9 days  | Not available |
| Other international | 7–15 days | Not available |

Oversized items ship by freight in 7–12 business days (Zones 1–3 only). Backordered and preorder items ship separately on their own schedule.

## Express delivery guarantee

If an express order arrives more than **1 business day** after its estimated delivery date, we refund the express shipping fee. Ask for it within 14 days of delivery. The guarantee doesn't cover weather delays or wrong addresses.

**Exception:** the express guarantee is suspended from **December 1 to December 24, 2026** because of peak holiday volume.

## 2026 holiday order deadlines

To get your order by December 24, 2026:

| Zone      | Standard order by | Express order by |
| --------- | ----------------- | ---------------- |
| Zones 1–2 | December 14       | December 18      |
| Zone 3    | December 10       | December 17      |
| Zone 4    | December 1        | Not available    |

These are best-effort dates, not guarantees.
