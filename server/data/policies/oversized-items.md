# Oversized Items

Oversized items are **kayaks, canoes, stand-up paddle (SUP) boards, bikes, and roof boxes**.

## Delivering oversized items

- Shipped by freight to **Zones 1–3 only**. Not available to Zone 4 or outside Canada.
- **$49 surcharge per item**, even if your order gets free shipping or you're a Summit member.
- Delivery takes **7–12 business days**. The carrier calls to book a delivery appointment.
- Delivery is **curbside only**.

## Picking up oversized items in store

You can pick up oversized items for free at our Calgary, Canmore, or Kamloops store. This is the only option for Zone 4 customers. Pickup orders are ready in 2–5 business days.

## Damage on delivery

Inspect the item while the driver is there and note any damage on the delivery receipt. Report it to us within **48 hours** (see Damaged Items policy).

## Returning oversized items

- Return window: 30 days from delivery. The item must be unused, with no water contact and no assembly beyond what's needed to inspect it.
- **20% restocking fee.**
- **$99 return freight fee** if we pick it up from you. There's no freight fee if you bring it to a store, but the restocking fee still applies.
- Both fees are waived if the item is defective or arrived damaged.
- Oversized items **can't be exchanged**. Return the item and place a new order.
